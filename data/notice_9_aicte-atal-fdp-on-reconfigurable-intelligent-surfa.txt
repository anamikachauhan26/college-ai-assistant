ABOUT THE INSTITUTE   
 
Walchand College of Engineering 
Sangli (WCE), established in 1947 and 
aided by the Government of 
Maharashtra, is one of the oldest and 
premier engineering institutions in 
India. With a rich history of over 70 
years and a beautiful campus of over 
90 acres, we are providing 
transformational learning experiences 
in various disciplines of engineering. 
WCE offers 4 diploma programs, 6 UG 
programs in Civil, Electrical, 
Mechanical, Electronics, Information 
technology, computer science, and 
engineering, and 10 PG p rograms. It 
also offers Ph.D. programs under 
Shivaji University, the National 
Doctoral Fellowship of AICTE, and the 
Quality Improvement Program (QIP) 
scheme of the Ministry of Human 
Resource Development (MHRD). At 
present, around 700 students are 
studying in Diploma programs, 1900 
students are studying in various 
B.Tech. programs, 480 students are in 
M.Tech. programs, and around 75 
scholars are pursuing their Ph.D. 
programs. WCE is affiliated with Shivaji 
University and is approved by AICTE, 
New Delhi. The institute has also been 
given autonomous status by the UGC 
since 2007. The alumni are spread 
across reputed Multinational and 
Indian companies such as Google, 
Microsoft, LinkedIn, Amazon, John 
Deere, Rakuten, Mahindra, TCS, and 
Infosys.

OBJECTIVE OF FDP 
The objective is to bring together 
cutting-edge research that advances 
our understanding of RIS -aided 
communications. The Objective of the 
workshops are 
 To introduce the fundamentals 
and working principles of RIS.  
 To explore RIS integrati on in 
modern wireless systems 
(5G/6G).  
 To understand channel modeling, 
beamforming, and optimization 
techniques.  
 To provide hands -on experience 
using simulation tools 
(HFSS/MATLAB).  
 To highlight research challenges 
and future directions in RIS 
technology 
BENEFITS AND OUTCOMES OF FDP 
Participants will be able to 
 Understand RIS fundamentals and 
system architecture,  
 Analyze RIS -assisted wireless 
communication systems, Design 
and simulate RIS-based models ,  
 Apply RIS concepts in real -world 
applications, Enabling precise 
control of electromagnetic waves 
to enhance communication, 
ensing, and localisation,  
 Understand high -speed data 
transmission, real -time health 
monitoring, and indoor 
localisation
