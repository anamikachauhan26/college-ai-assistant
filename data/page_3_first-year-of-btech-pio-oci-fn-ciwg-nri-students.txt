First Year of BTech–PIO/OCI/FN/ CIWGC/NRI Students
Walchand College of Engineering also offers admissions in First Year Four Year Course to Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) , Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) , Foreign Nationals (FN,) Children of Gulf Workers ( CIWGC) and Non-Resident Indian (NRI) candidates. All such eligible candidates are required to apply separately through the Institute’s website. The admission process for PIO/OCI/FN/ CIWGC/NRI candidates is based on their performance in the qualifying examination (equivalent to HSC/Std. XII) and other criteria as specified by the Institute.

Important Note:
This form is strictly for students applying under the NRI / FN / PIO / OCI category for B.Tech admissions at Walchand College of Engineering. Students who do not belong to any of the above categories are requested not to fill out this form.
Eligibility
Candidate whose name appears in the confirmation list, declared by the CET Cell, Maharashtra State, Mumbai for Admission to First Year of Under Graduate Technical Courses in Engineering and Technology for CIWGC, PIO/OCI/FN and NRI are only eligible to appear for admission process as per institute guideline to First Year of Under Graduate Technical Courses in Engineering and Technology under CIWGC, PIO/OCI/FN and NRI category at Walchand College of Engineering.
Seats/Capacity
Fifteen percent (15%) seats in the institution are allowed on supernumerary basis from amongst Non-Residential Indian (NRI) / Persons of Indian Origin (PIO)/ Overseas Citizen of Indian (OCI) / Foreign Nationals (FN) and Children of Indian Workers in Gulf Countries (CIWGC) over and above of the approved intake of each discipline as sanctioned by DTE, Government of Maharashtra from time to time.
