B.Tech Electronics Engineering Curriculum
Curriculum:
The B.Tech curriculum in Electronics Engineering is designed to provide students with a comprehensive education in Electronics related courses. In the Academic Year 2024-2025 curriculum of First year and Second Year is implemented as per NEP 2020 policy which includes multidisciplinary minor (MDM) as well. Electronics department offers MDM in Electronics and (14 credits).
Outcome-Based Educational Philosophy in Electronics Engineering
Focus on Learning Outcomes:
Program Outcomes (POs):
Broad skills and knowledge students should have after graduation (e.g., problem-solving, design skills, ethics).
Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs):
Competencies specific to Electronics Engineering (e.g., circuit analysis, embedded system design).
Course Outcomes (COs):
What students are expected to learn at the end of each course.
Backward Curriculum Design:
Starts with defining desired graduate attributes and outcomes, and then designs the curriculum, teaching methods, and assessments to meet these goals.
Continuous Assessment & Feedback:
Direct Assessments:
Assignments, lab work, exams mapped to COs and POs.
Indirect Assessments:
Surveys, feedback from students, alumni, and employers.
Regular monitoring ensures course and program improvements.
Industry-Relevant Skills:
Electronics Engineering under OBE emphasizes:
Hands-on design and simulation (e.g., using tools like MATLAB, PSPICE)
Industry projects and internships
Skills in VLSI, IoT, Embedded Systems, and Communication technologies
Lifelong Learning & Ethics:
Encourages self-learning, innovation, and ethical responsibility.
Integrates modern tools and interdisciplinary knowledge.
Student-Centric Learning:
Promotes active learning, peer collaboration, and problem-solving.
Encourages participation in mini-projects, hackathons, and design challenges.
