Walchand College of Engineering admits students to its undergraduate B. Tech in directly Second Year of Four Year Course (DSE)  through the Centralised Admission  Process (CAP) conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra.
Aided and Unaided Programs
Walchand College of Engineering offers both aided and unaided B. Tech Programs. Aided courses are basically financed by Government of Maharashtra. Since institute is partially aided institute (90% of salary of aided courses) , teacher and staff salary to large extent is supported from government aid ( fund) , hence tuition fees of such courses are lesser than unaided courses. Unaided courses are primarily dependent on student fees for salary and other expenses , so tuition fees are on higher side.

Eligibility Criteria for DSY after Diploma / BSc
To be eligible for admission to Walchand College of Engineering, candidates must satisfy the following conditions:
Passed the Diploma examination of Maharashtra State Board of Technical Education (MSBTE) OR its equivalent examination in relevant courses of study.
Obtained a minimum of 50% marks (45% for backward class candidates from Maharashtra) in the subjects Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics added together.
For Domicile and other conditions , one should refer CAP / CET cell guidelines.

Centralised Admission Process (CAP)
The Centralised Admission Process (CAP) is conducted by the State Common Entrance Test Cell, Maharashtra. The admission process involves the following steps:
Registration_: Candidates must register online for CAP on the official website.
Document Verification_: Candidates must verify their documents at designated Facilitation Centres (FCs).
Option Form Filling_: Candidates must fill in their preferred college and course options in multiple rounds ( CAP1, CAP2, CAP3 etc)
Seat Allocation_: Seats are allocated to candidates based on their merit and preferences.
Reporting to College_: Candidates must report to the allocated college to confirm their admission once student confirms allotment through CAP

Note for TFWS/ EWS Seats
There shall be supernumerary ( in addition to above intake capacity ) seats under Tuition Fee Wavier scheme and economically weaker section as per directions of AICTE/ GoI/ GoM.
