Overview: Department of Information Technology
Information Technology (IT) Department was established in 2001 with an Under-Graduate (UG) programme and then Post Graduate (PG) was started in 2012. Both UG and PG programmes are NBA accredited. The curriculum contains professional core courses, professional elective courses, open elective and professional elective has five tracks. The track system provides the students; the flexibility to choose the elective courses based on the aptitude, interests and career plan of students. Courses and course contents are designed by taking inputs from Industry Consultation Committee (ICC).
Department offers Certifications for Minor in Artificial Intelligence-Machine Learning (AI-ML) and Web Development (WD), Honor in Block-chain Technology (BCT), Software Project Development (SPD), IT System & Management (ITSM) and Value Added Courses (VAC) like Cloud Computing: Industry Driven Approach, Machine Learning using Python and Responsive Web Design. IT Department also provides support for research guidance to Ph.D. (CSE) of Shivaji University, Kolhapur, under ADF and QIP schemes.
Department has consistent performance in academics and placement. Department has highly equipped and updated nine laboratories for UG and PG. Department has received various research projects/funding’s from National/International agencies like DRDO/ AICTE/ DST/ NVidia/etc. Department best practices are- e-learning motivations through Open Electives and professional Electives form MOOC (massively open online courses), 24×7 Lab availability lab with 800Mbps internet facility, use of Free & Open Source Software and Tools to learn the curriculum. Students are engaged in various co-curricular and extracurricular activities of SAIT, WLUG, PACE, etc.
Objectives
The Department of Information Technology is dedicated to shaping competent IT professionals who can contribute meaningfully to the digital world. The department strives to achieve the following objectives:
Deliver Quality IT Education
Provide a robust and contemporary curriculum that imparts theoretical knowledge and practical skills in information technology, preparing students for careers in software development, systems management, data analytics, and beyond.
Promote Innovation and Research
Encourage students and faculty to engage in cutting-edge research and innovative projects in areas such as cloud computing, IoT, cyber security, block chain, AI, and mobile applications.
Build Industry-Ready Talent
Equip students with hands-on experience, domain knowledge, and professional skills to meet the dynamic needs of the IT industry through labs, certifications, internships, and industry-linked projects.
Foster Lifelong Learning and Adaptability
The importance of continuous professional development and adaptability to emerging technologies and industry trends through workshops, online learning, and certifications.
Enhance Industry Collaboration
Collaborate with IT companies, start-ups, and research institutions to offer students real-world exposure, internships, joint research opportunities, and employment prospects.
Encourage Entrepreneurial Thinking
Support and guide students in transforming innovative ideas into viable business solutions by promoting entrepreneurship and start up incubation.
Promote Ethical and Social Responsibility
Develop responsible IT professionals who uphold ethical values and contribute to solving societal problems through the application of technology.
Strive for Academic Excellence
Continuously enhances teaching-learning practices, faculty development, and infrastructure to maintain high academic standards and improve student outcomes.

Leadership
Dr. Rathod avindra Ramkishan
(Head of the Department of Information Technology)
Dr. Rathod Ravindra Ramkishan
is the Head of the Department of Information Technology at Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli. He has been a faculty member at the institute since 2004 and has contributed extensively to the development of the IT department through his teaching, research, and academic leadership. Dr. Rathod earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee, and completed his M.E. from Walchand College of Engineering and B.E. from MGM’s College of Engineering, Nanded.
His research interests span data mining, geographic information systems (GIS), artificial intelligence, and image processing. He has published several research papers in reputed international journals and conferences and holds one published patent with FER submission in 2022. Dr. Rathod has actively participated in faculty development, having attended over 25 weeks of FDPs and organized two. He has also completed multiple NPTEL/SWAYAM courses to continuously update his knowledge and pedagogy.
In recognition of his excellence in teaching, Dr. Rathod received the prestigious “Ideal Teacher Commendable Prize” in 2009 from Walchand College of Engineering. He remains actively involved in academic mentoring and curriculum development, and is a valued member of the research community, with profiles on Google Scholar, ORCID, and Scopus. His dedication and contributions have established him as a respected academic and mentor within the institute.
