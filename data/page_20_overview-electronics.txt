Overview: Department of Electronics Engineering
The
Department of Electronics Engineering
focuses on the study, design, and application of electronic systems and technologies that power modern communication, automation, computing, and control systems. It integrates theoretical foundations with hands-on practical skills to prepare students for dynamic careers in industry, research, and entrepreneurship.

Key Highlights :
Established: 1986 (UG & PG); recognized for Diploma in Industrial Electronics
Accreditations: NBA-accredited; PhD center under AICTE, Shivaji University (QIP, ADF Scheme)
Programs Offered:
Diploma: Industrial Electronics (40 seats)
B.Tech: Electronics Engineering (60 seats)
M.Tech: Electronics Engineering (12 seats)
The department has a rich legacy of accomplishments, including
Academic Distinction:
Consistent performance in university rankings and high success rates in GATE, GRE, TOEFL, and competitive examinations.
Strong Placement Record:
Alumni placed in prestigious organizations such as Intel, John Deere, Cadence, L&T, Infosys, TCS, Siemens, and DRDO, across both core and software domains.
Research and Publications:
Faculty and students actively contribute to national and international journals (Springer, IEEE, Wiley) and participate in funded research under AICTE, BARC, and MODROB.
Advanced Laboratories:
State-of-the-art facilities including Embedded Systems Lab, VLSI Lab, Communication Lab, Power Electronics Lab, and the John Deere Research Lab support innovation and experimentation.
Industry Collaborations:
Productive partnerships with companies through internships, sponsored projects, and centers of excellence like the IBM Software Lab and John Deere Research Cell.
Student Achievements:
Student teams have excelled in national contests such as Smart India Hackathon, e-Yantra, Avishkar, and IEEE Project Competitions, demonstrating practical problem-solving abilities.
Alumni Network:
A strong and active alumni base making global contributions in academia, R&D, entrepreneurship, and Fortune 500 companies.
Higher Education Pathways:
Graduates have pursued advanced studies at reputed institutions like IITs, IISc, NITs, and universities abroad.
Vision
To be an Electronics Engineering program of the first choice by the aspiring students and prospective employers by implementing world class education practices.
Mission
To meet above Vision, all stakeholders of this program are committed towards Outcome Based Education Philosophy by:
Adopting vibrant academic curricula and implementing innovative teaching learning processes.
Providing opportunities to the students for the development of professional skills.
Nurturing critical thinking and creativity in students.
Inculcating in students the life-long learning attitude and sensitivity towards society & environment.

The Department of Electronics Engineering aims to achieve:
Academic Excellence:
To impart robust and contemporary education in electronics engineering, combining theoretical knowledge with practical competencies through hands-on learning, modern laboratories, and project-based pedagogy.
Innovation & Research:
To cultivate a research-driven environment encouraging innovation, interdisciplinary exploration, and the development of cutting-edge solutions for societal and industrial challenges.
Industry-Readiness:
To equip students with the skills, tools, and exposure needed to thrive in core electronics, embedded systems, VLSI design, IoT, and automation domains through internships, certifications, and live projects in collaboration with industry partners.
Entrepreneurship & Leadership:
To nurture entrepreneurial spirit, problem-solving ability, and leadership qualities by supporting student-led startups, innovation cells, and participation in national and international competitions.
Social & Ethical Responsibility:
To produce engineers who are ethically grounded, socially conscious, and committed to using technology for sustainable and inclusive development.
Global Competence & Lifelong Learning:
To inspire students to pursue lifelong learning, adapt to global technological trends, and contribute to the international scientific and engineering community.
Leadership
Dr. Sachin D. Ruikar
(Head of Electronics Engineering Department)
He has completed PhD in Electronics under Shri Guru Gobind Singh Institute of Engineering Technology, SRTMU Nanded, in 2013. He has received postgraduate degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Government Engineering College, Pune University, India in 2001. He has received Graduate degree in Electronics and Telecommunication from Government Engineering College, Aurangabad, under the aegis of Dr. B. A. M. U., Aurangabad, in 1998. He has a distinguished research background spanning over years in the domains of Microwave Communication, Antenna Design, Satellite Communication, Radar and Navigation, Optical Communication, Mobile Communication and Internet of Things (IoT). Their scholarly contributions include many publications in reputed national and international journals and conferences.  He has guided numerous postgraduate and doctoral students and is actively involved in interdisciplinary research projects.
The Head of the Electronics Engineering Department demonstrates visionary leadership and unwavering commitment to academic excellence, research innovation, and holistic student development. With a deep understanding of evolving technological trends and industry demands, the HOD strategically guides the department towards its mission of producing competent and responsible engineering professionals. Under his leadership, the department has strengthened industry-academia collaboration through MoUs, industrial visits, internships, and guest lectures, promoted a culture of research and innovation. The department faculty and students has encouraged to publish papers, participate in technical competitions, and pursue funded projects. The department has focused on outcome-based education (OBE) and continuous curriculum enhancement to align with national and global standards. As a mentor and guide, the HOD plays a pivotal role in empowering faculty and students to reach their highest potential. Their leadership is instrumental in shaping the department into a centre of academic and technical excellence.
